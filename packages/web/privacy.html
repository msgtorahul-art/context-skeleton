Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

ContextSkeleton operates 100% locally on your machine. We do not transmit, collect, store, or analyze your source code, AST structures, repository contents, or LLM prompt data.

2. Local Execution & Privacy Guarantee

All AST folding, Tree-Sitter parsing, and token estimation calculations are performed entirely in local memory via Wasm and client-side JavaScript. No data ever leaves your device.

3. Web Analytics

Our website utilizes zero tracking cookies. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy, please contact privacy@contextskeleton.com.
