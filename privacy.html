Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026 | Published by FunctionApp (functionapp.in)

1. Zero Telemetry & Local Air-Gapped Execution

ContextSkeleton is built with a zero-data collection policy. When you use the ContextSkeleton CLI binary (`context-skeleton scan`) or Model Context Protocol (`context-skeleton-mcp`), 100% of structural code folding occurs locally on your machine. Your source code is never transmitted to any external servers, third-party APIs, or analytics databases.

2. Website Inquiries

If you submit an enterprise contact inquiry form on functionapp.in or contextskeleton.com, we collect only the contact information provided (such as work email and organization size) solely to respond to your request. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

3. Open Source Transparency

ContextSkeleton is open-source software licensed under the MIT License. You can audit our codebase directly on GitHub at github.com/msgtorahul-art/context-skeleton.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding privacy or compliance, reach out via FunctionApp's official platform at functionapp.in.
