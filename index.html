How It Works

Engineered for zero latency, precision signature retention, and high compression.

Step 01

Parse

Fast zero-dependency scanner parses source code across JS/TS, Python, Go, and Rust to identify exported signatures, structs, types, and docstrings.

⚡ Parsing Speed: ~0.4ms
Step 02

Fold

Selectively folds internal implementation bodies into comment markers while preserving 100% syntactically intact signatures and docstrings.

📊 Blended Savings: ~67%
Step 03

Serve

Outputs compressed context directly to CLI stdout or streams over MCP stdio to Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, or AGY agents.

🔌 Stdio Latency: Sub-1ms
